fix(lifecycle): stop the thunderstorm flash when Reduce motion is on

The flash is the one animation in the app the stylesheet cannot reach: every
other animation follows prefers-reduced-motion through a media query, and the
particle field follows it through the `still` flag, but lightning() builds its
effect with Element.animate() and never looked at the setting. A reader who
asked the system for no motion — and who by the same setting got no rain, no
snow, no parallax and no springs — still had the whole screen flash white every
2.5-7.5 seconds in a storm, with a timer waking the WebView to start each one.

lightning() now returns after clearing its timer while the setting is on, and
onMotionChange() calls it in both directions, so turning the setting off
mid-storm restarts the chain and turning it back on stops it.

Measured in headless Chromium with the query emulated: 3 flashes in 14s before,
0 after, with the same storm under no-preference still flashing (2 vs 4 — the
interval is random) and the frame loop unchanged (rAF 104 vs 102). #main's
markup and every computed style of the bar, hero, cards, nav, pill, photo layer,
active scene and the --k/--glass/--blur/--dp/--pr/--cc variables are identical
to 1.8.9 across four payloads, so nothing on screen changes for anyone else.
